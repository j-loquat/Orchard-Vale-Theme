## How The Kit Was Made

- The HTML, CSS, JavaScript, documentation, naming system, layout composition, and asset organization were authored for Orchard Vale.
- The raster artwork was created with AI-assisted image generation and then selected, processed, normalized, keyed, cropped, or composed for this theme kit.
- The SVG interface assets were created as reusable Orchard Vale controls, icons, motifs, ornaments, and data-visualization pieces.
- Files under `references/` preserve the original Orchard Vale concept mock-ups used to define and evaluate the visual language.
